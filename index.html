05 · Global From Day One

Built in India.
Sold to the world.

Beyond India, Nuvora lists directly on Amazon's global storefronts — one brand, four marketplaces, immediate international reach.

Compact, flat-pack design isn't just an India cost advantage — it's an export advantage. The same low-dimensional-weight products that undercut offline India pricing are cheap to freight abroad, letting Nuvora list globally without a second manufacturing base.
